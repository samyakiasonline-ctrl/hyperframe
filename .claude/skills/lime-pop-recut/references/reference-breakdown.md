## Measurements

- Frame luma average: 45–58/255 the whole video (moody), flash peak 188.
- Hero cap height ≈ 75 px at 720 w → font ≈ 155 px at 1080 w. `Understand` spans 80 % of the width.
- Lead/tail font ≈ 0.38–0.55 × hero. The tail under the REC hero is smallest (≈ 0.35×).
- Ring diameter: about 425 px at 720 w once settled (59 % of width, ≈ 640 px at 1080), entering from about 570 px. Centre at 40 % height. Stroke ≈ 3 px at 720.
- Alert card ≈ 445×82 px at 720 (62 % width), radius ≈ 20 px. Card gap ≈ 45 px.
- Bottom 100 px (at 720) is pure black: a framing gradient.
- Cuts by luma/scene detection: 3.13, 4.40 (flash), 6.20, 8.10. By audio silence: 3.0, 7.9–8.1, 11.4, 13.7–14.0, 15.3.

## Why it works (editorial reading)

- **Hero-word captions** let the viewer read the line in one glance (5–6 heroes in 17 s), with
  small words for grammar. Sound-off viewers still get the whole argument.
- **Props make the abstract literal**: "failing" becomes a strike, "record" a REC frame, "unsure"
  error toasts, "stress" a grimace in a spotlight ring. Each prop arrives on its word.
- **No music** keeps it feeling like a direct, honest talk. The few SFX land like punctuation.
- **The resolution beat is undressed**: after 10 s of red UI, a clean lime `Your plate` reads as relief.
